## 🌿 What is LocalLink?

**LocalLink** is a local-services discovery platform designed around one simple problem:

> **“I need someone nearby who can help me.”**

Instead of searching through scattered contacts or travelling to a bigger town for basic services, users can discover local professionals, check useful details, and contact or request a service from one place.

The project is designed specifically with **small towns, villages and nearby communities** in mind, with a strong focus on simplicity and accessibility.

## 🗺️ Local Coverage

LocalLink is designed around the **Nurpur region of Kangra, Himachal Pradesh**, including:

`Nurpur` · `Suliali` · `Sadwan` · `Jassur` · `Kandwal` · `Rehan` · `Gangath` · `Raja Ka Talab` · `Damtal`

Providers can also specify multiple areas they serve, making the platform realistic for professionals who travel between nearby villages.
